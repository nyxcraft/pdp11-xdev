pdp11-ranlib

Builds the __.SYMDEF table of contents in an archive, so ld resolves members regardless of order — the canonical V7/2.9 ranlib paired with our ar.

Builds the __.SYMDEF table of contents in an archive, so ld resolves members regardless of order — the canonical V7/2.9 ranlib paired with our ar.

pdp11-ranlib archive ...
  • __.SYMDEF content matches the native ranlib (run end-to-end under apsim) on all 26 ranlib'd libraries of the 2.9 tree — only the embedded timestamp is nondeterministic.
  • Middle-endian offsets: the symbol-definition file's member offsets are PDP-11 longs, written through the same PDPL() swap ar and ld use.
