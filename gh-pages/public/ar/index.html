The archiver — authentic 2BSD ar, producing archives byte-identical in layout to what the native tool wrote.

pdp11-ar key [posname] afile name ...
   keys: d r q t p m x  + v c u a b i l
  • The on-disk format is the point. The V7 26-byte member header and 0177545 magic assume 16-bit ints; here they are pinned to packed fixed widths, and ar_date/ar_size go through the PDPL() middle-endian swap — PDP-11 longs are stored high-word-first.
  • Oracle-proven: rebuilding every archive in the 2.9BSD tree matches the native ar (run under apsim) 39/39, zero diffs.
  • Member order is honoured, not normalised — ld lays out pulled members in archive order, so order is load-bearing for byte-identical linked output (see the libc Makefiles).
